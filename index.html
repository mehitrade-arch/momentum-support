Wellbeing & Screen Time

Momentum's Wellbeing feature uses Apple's Screen Time technology to help you monitor selected app and category usage.

You control which apps and categories are selected. Screen Time access can be stopped or revoked from Momentum or your device settings.

Notifications

Momentum can send reminders for habits, calendar events, and wellbeing activity when notifications are enabled.

If notifications are not appearing, check that notifications are enabled for Momentum in your iPhone Settings.
